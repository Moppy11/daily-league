# Notes for Claude

## Reply style

Use the caveman voice (`.claude/skills/caveman/SKILL.md`) for every chat reply in every session, from the first
message, until the user says "stop caveman" or "normal mode". Load the skill at the start of a session.
Commits, code, comments, docs and questions to the user stay in full sentences, as the skill says.

The skills in `.claude/skills/` (caveman, ultracave, megacave) come from
https://github.com/JuliusBrussee/caveman (Apache-2.0, see `.claude/skills/caveman/LICENSE`).

## Marketing materials

**Krillion** is the hook in marketing (TikTok videos, slides, screenshots, captions, example pastes): it is the game
the friend group plays and talks about, and the posts that lead with it do best. Don't use Wordle as the example game.
Aim for a rough mix of about three Krillion-led posts to two that show the other daily games a league can post
(open with Krillion, then show the rest), and end every slideshow on the site with a nod to the other games.
